# Phase 1: complete tooltip texts

All 33 info icons were checked on hover and keyboard focus in the browser. Duplicate texts are retained here so every location is explicit. The eight number inputs and eight paired sliders use the same help as their control icon. Chart hover/focus values remain available.

| Location / label | Exact tooltip text |
| --- | --- |
| Headline KPI: Net sales | Product sales after returns in the last 12 months, for customers we can identify. This is revenue, not profit. |
| Headline KPI: Customers | Customers who bought in the last 12 months. The note below shows all retained accounts, including those who have not bought recently. |
| Headline KPI: Returns | The share of invoices that record a return in the last 12 months. A return may cover only part of an order. |
| Headline KPI: Expected profit from the campaign | Extra profit we expect from the proposed campaign after its costs. It depends on our assumptions about offer responses and margin. |
| Who is likely to buy? header: Customer | The customer’s identifier in the source data. |
| Who is likely to buy? header: Days since last purchase | Days from the customer’s last recorded purchase to 9 December 2011. |
| Who is likely to buy? header: Purchases | The number of days on which this customer bought something in the retained history. Several orders on one day count as one purchase. |
| Who is likely to buy? header: Customer age (days) | Days from the customer’s first recorded purchase to 9 December 2011. This is the length of their recorded history, not their personal age. |
| Who is likely to buy? header: Probability of buying · 90 days | The estimated chance that the customer buys at least once in the next 90 days, even if we do nothing. It is not a guarantee. |
| Who is likely to buy? header: Expected spend · 90 days | What we expect the customer to spend in the next 90 days even if we do nothing. |
| Campaign control: Reset proposal | Restore the starting budget, coupon limit and assumptions for the proposal. |
| Campaign control: Budget (£) | The most we are willing to spend on emails and coupons. |
| Campaign control: Maximum coupons | A ceiling on coupons, not a target. |
| Campaign control: Advanced assumptions | Change the assumed margin, offer responses, costs and coupon discount used in the proposal. |
| Campaign control: Margin (assumption, %) | The share of each pound of sales left after product costs, before campaign costs. This is an assumption, not measured from these data. |
| Campaign control: Response to email (assumption, %) | The assumed share of customers who would not otherwise buy but buy because of an email. An A/B test would help estimate this. |
| Campaign control: Response to coupon (assumption, %) | The assumed share of customers who would not otherwise buy but buy because of a coupon. An A/B test would help estimate this. |
| Campaign control: Email cost (£) | The cost of sending one email, whether or not the customer buys. |
| Campaign control: Coupon fixed cost (£) | The fixed cost of offering one coupon, before any discount on a purchase. |
| Campaign control: Coupon discount (%) | The share taken off the purchase price when a customer uses the coupon. |
| Campaign KPI: No contact | Customers we propose leaving without an email or coupon. No messages are sent from this demo. |
| Campaign KPI: Email | Customers we propose sending an email to. This is a proposed action, not a confirmed purchase. |
| Campaign KPI: Coupon | Customers we propose offering a coupon to. This is a proposed action, not a confirmed purchase. |
| Campaign KPI: Total expected cost | What we expect to spend on emails, coupon costs and discounts for this proposal. It can be less than the budget. |
| Campaign KPI: Expected profit | Extra profit we expect from the proposed campaign after its costs. It depends on our assumptions about offer responses and margin. |
| Campaign KPI: Return on investment (ROI) | Expected extra profit divided by expected campaign cost. For example, £5.43 means £5.43 profit for each £1 spent. It depends on our assumptions. |
| Customers to reactivate header: Customer | The customer’s identifier in the source data. |
| Customers to reactivate header: Recommended action | The action proposed for this customer under the current budget, coupon limit and assumptions. |
| Customers to reactivate header: Days since last purchase | Days from the customer’s last recorded purchase to 9 December 2011. |
| Customers to reactivate header: Expected spend · 90 days | What we expect the customer to spend in the next 90 days even if we do nothing. |
| Customers to reactivate header: Expected gain | Extra profit we expect from this action after its cost; it depends on our assumption of how customers respond to the offer. |
| Historical check KPI: Contacted-customer realized spend | What the customers selected by this proposal actually spent in the historical check. These purchases happened without this campaign. |
| Historical check KPI: Share of eligible spend | The share of all eligible customers’ actual spending covered by the selected customers in the historical check. It does not show extra sales caused by a campaign. |
